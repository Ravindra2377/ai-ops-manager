# Privacy Policy for Loopback

**Last Updated**: February 8, 2026

## Introduction

Loopback ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our email management application.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Account Information
- Email address (for authentication)
- Name (optional, for personalization)
- Password (encrypted and hashed)

### 2. Gmail Data
When you connect your Gmail account, we access:
- Email metadata (sender, subject, date, labels)
- Email content (for AI analysis only)
- Gmail labels and categories

**Important**: We only access emails you explicitly grant permission to read. We never send emails on your behalf without your explicit action.

### 3. Usage Data
- Email priority classifications
- Decision follow-through actions
- Reminder preferences
- App usage analytics (anonymized)

## How We Use Your Information

### Core Functionality
- **AI Email Analysis**: Classify emails by intent and urgency
- **Daily Brief Generation**: Summarize your inbox state
- **Decision Follow-Through**: Track commitments you make via email
- **Smart Reminders**: Notify you about important emails

### Service Improvement
- Improve AI classification accuracy
- Optimize notification timing
- Debug technical issues
- Analyze feature usage (anonymized)

## Data Storage and Security

### Storage
- **Database**: MongoDB Atlas (encrypted at rest)
- **Email Data**: Stored temporarily for analysis, deleted after 90 days
- **Gmail Tokens**: Encrypted using industry-standard encryption
- **Server**: Hosted on Render.com (SOC 2 compliant)

### Security Measures
- All data transmitted over HTTPS/TLS
- Passwords hashed using bcrypt
- OAuth 2.0 for Gmail authentication
- Regular security audits
- No third-party data sharing

## Data Retention

- **Active Users**: Data retained while account is active
- **Inactive Accounts**: Data deleted after 180 days of inactivity
- **Deleted Accounts**: All data permanently deleted within 30 days
- **Email Content**: Deleted after 90 days or when you disconnect Gmail

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access**: Request a copy of your data
- **Delete**: Delete your account and all associated data
- **Disconnect**: Revoke Gmail access at any time
- **Export**: Download your data in JSON format
- **Opt-out**: Disable specific features (reminders, notifications)

### How to Exercise Your Rights
- **In-App**: Settings → Privacy → Manage Data
- **Email**: privacy@loopback.app (we'll respond within 7 days)

## Cookies and Tracking

We use minimal tracking:
- **Authentication Tokens**: To keep you logged in
- **Session Cookies**: For app functionality
- **No Advertising Cookies**: We don't track you for ads
- **No Third-Party Analytics**: No Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, etc.

## Children's Privacy

Loopback is not intended for users under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us immediately.

## International Users

Loopback is hosted in the United States. By using our service, you consent to the transfer of your data to the US. We comply with applicable data protection laws including GDPR and CCPA.

### GDPR Rights (EU Users)
- Right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, and port your data
- Right to object to processing
- Right to withdraw consent
- Right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority

### CCPA Rights (California Users)

- Right to delete personal information
